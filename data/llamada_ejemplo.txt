AGENT: Good morning, thanks for calling Riverside Auto Repair, this is Maria speaking.
CUSTOMER: Hi Maria, my car is making a grinding noise when I brake.
AGENT: I'm sorry to hear that. How long has it been happening?
CUSTOMER: About a week now, and it's getting louder.
AGENT: That sounds like it could be the brake pads. We can take a look today at two o'clock.
CUSTOMER: Two works for me. How much does an inspection cost?
AGENT: The brake inspection is free, and we'll give you a quote before any work.
CUSTOMER: Great, please book me in. My name is David Lee.
AGENT: Perfect, David. You're booked for two o'clock today. See you then.
CUSTOMER: Thank you, bye.
